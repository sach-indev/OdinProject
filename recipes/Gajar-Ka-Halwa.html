Gajar Ka Halwa

When winter arrives, the craving for something warm, sweet, and comforting hits instantly—and nothing beats a bowl of Gajar Ka Halwa. At Mala Flavors, we love celebrating traditional Indian desserts that remind us of home, and this one tops the list! Whether you’re a beginner or a pro in the kitchen, this simple and rich gajar ka halwa recipe will make your winter extra delicious.


Ingredients

  • Red carrots (grated)
  • Full-fat milk
  • Ghee
  • Sugar
  • Cardamom powder
  • Dry fruits (almonds, cashews, raisins)

Instructions

  1. Prep the Carrots
    Wash, peel, and grate fresh red carrots. The fresher the carrots, the sweeter the halwa.

  2. Cook with Milk
    Add the grated carrots to a pan, pour in the milk, and cook them on medium flame until the milk reduces completely.

  3. Add Ghee & Sugar
    Once the milk is absorbed, add ghee and sauté the mixture. Add sugar and cook until the halwa thickens.

  4. Add Flavor
    Mix in cardamom powder and chopped dry fruits.

  5. Serve Warm
    Your delicious Gajar Ka Halwa is ready to enjoy!
